## Key Finding

V2 provides stronger structure, better control over answer depth,
and clearer examination-oriented formatting than the baseline prompt.

However, equation rendering and technical verification still require
improvement.

## Prompt Engineering Insight

Adding explicit structure and constraints improves consistency,
but detailed instructions alone do not guarantee correct equation
rendering or technical accuracy.

Further prompt iteration is therefore required.
